# Product Spec — template

The product spec states what the product is for and where it stops. It sits at the top of
the sources of truth: every feature contract is a projection of it, and code that
contradicts it is the bug. It lives at `docs/product-spec.md`; its existence puts the
project in full methodology.

It is authoritative only once the human validates it. The Product Owner role drafts it
from a dialogue with the human; it never invents the vision on the human's behalf.

Keep it to one page. It is a product statement, not a backlog.

---

## For whom (required)
The people the product serves, specifically enough to say who it is NOT for.

## Problem (required)
The precise pain the product removes, in the users' terms.

## What the product does (required)
The core capabilities, each stated as an observable outcome for the user. A short list.

## What the product will NOT do (required)
Explicit boundaries. Name the adjacent problems, audiences and features deliberately left
out. A product with no stated boundaries is a product that will sprawl.

## Success measures (required)
The measurable outcomes that tell whether the product works. No measure, no way to judge
a feature's worth.

## Open questions
Undecided product questions, each flagged until the human decides it. A decided question
moves into the sections above; its decision is logged in the journal.
